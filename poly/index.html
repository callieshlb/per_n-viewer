How this is computed, and the checks

c, the characteristic angles, internal address, denominators, limb and kneading come from the E90 census; this page's data (poly_info.json, made by poly_info.py) recomputes the combinatorics in exact arithmetic and must reproduce them. Kneading (Lau–Schleicher): νk = 1 if 2k−1θ ∈ (θ/2, (θ+1)/2), 0 outside, * on the boundary. Internal address: the orbit of 1 under ρ(r) = min{k > r : νk ≠ νk−r}; angles by Bruin–Schleicher. Rays: Newton on the Böttcher equation, then continued to the landing point by the branch of f−n fixing it. Tree: a spider from each xk to the root of its Fatou component, lifted through z ↦ ±√(z − c) to convergence; its endpoints are checked against E91's combinatorial leaf test.
