External rays
Angles as p/q or decimals, separated by commas, e.g. 1/3, 2/3, 1/7, 1/6. Parameter rays are drawn here; the dynamic rays of the same angles are drawn in the dynamical plane (for the selected c). Rational angles get their exact landing point (a root, or a Misiurewicz point).
