What this is, and what is exact

The hyperbolic metric was computed on the glued mesh of the genus-4 curve (discrete conformal equivalence, Bobenko–Pinkall–Springborn), the surface developed into the disk, and its Fuchsian group read off from the side pairings. The tile is the Dirichlet polygon of that group about a base point: faces with the same number and color are glued. Corners of the same color are one point of the surface.

Live coloring: each pixel's point is located in one of 343 conformal charts (centers spread over the surface so every point is within 0.24 of one), which store y = 1/(s − 1) (s = a + b) and x′ = s(s + 1)2a y4; x′ is then recomputed as the root of the monic quartic Q(x′, y) = 0 nearest the stored value (Durand–Kerner + Newton, in single precision on the GPU), a = x′/(y4s(s + 1)2), b = s − a, and the free critical point ω′ = −2a/b of f(z) = 1 + b/z + a/z2 is iterated in single precision on the GPU (captured by the landing fixed point w5: color as the E96 PrePer panes). Pixels near the view center are read from the chart of the view center directly (no folding), which keeps zooms sharp. Very deep zooms reach the limits of single precision and of the chart data.

Up to discretization error the picture is conformal: hyperbolic components keep their shape when moved to the center. Checks (angle sums, area 12π, side pairings, mesh convergence, real circles geodesic, chart consistency) are in REGISTRATION.md (PP51).

Real circles (cyan, yellow): the two real circles of PrePer5,1 (real s in [−2.853, −0.656] and [−0.410, 0.389]); they are closed geodesics of this metric (PP51-U6), drawn here as exact geodesics.
