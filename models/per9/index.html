What this is, and what is exact

The hyperbolic metric was computed on the glued mesh of the genus-188 curve (discrete conformal equivalence, Bobenko–Pinkall–Springborn), the surface developed into the disk, and its Fuchsian group read off from the side pairings. The tile is the Dirichlet polygon of that group about a base point: faces with the same number and color are glued. Corners of the same color are one point of the surface.

Live coloring: each pixel's point is located in one of 804 conformal charts (centers spread over the surface so every point is within 1.2 of one; the pixel walks from chart to neighbouring chart until it reaches the nearest center), which store a3 and a8; the free critical point is then iterated in single precision on the GPU from the chart values (no Newton polishing on the GPU at degree 42; the "Point" panel tries a double-precision polish and keeps the chart value when Newton does not settle, which at degree 42 is the usual case). Where |a3| < 0.04 the picture is masked: a8 was not computed there. Pixels near the view center are read from the chart of the view center directly (no folding), which keeps zooms sharp. Very deep zooms reach the limits of single precision and of the chart data.

Up to discretization error the picture is conformal: hyperbolic components keep their shape when moved to the center. Checks (angle sums, area 748π, side pairings, mesh convergence) are in REGISTRATION.md (E95).

Real circles: 2 of the 6 traced real circles are drawn (cyan, yellow; census ovals 4, 5, closed geodesics of lengths 8.873, 7.021, each through 3 punctures): those that pass check U6. The other 4 do not pass and are not drawn; the 6 traced circles are a lower bound for the real locus (8 real arcs did not land). G9 edges (white, off by default): the 933 pairs of centers joined in G9 (E91 construction), each drawn as the shortest geodesic between them.

Click a marker (white dot or red dot) to see its data: for a polynomial center z2 + c its c, characteristic angles, internal address and kneading; for a puncture the centers sharing it, k, q, multiplicity, its census loop point and how it was placed (internal ray of a polynomial component).
