What are the tubes?

Per5(0) as drawn here is its normalization, a smooth torus. Each point is a quadratic rational map whose critical point 0 has exact period 5, together with that marking.

Nodes. At a bitransitive map both critical points lie on the 5-cycle, so the map appears twice on Per5(0), once with each critical point marked. Forgetting the marking (passing to the moduli space M2) glues the two points together, and the image curve has a node there. The 20 bitransitive points form 10 pairs: within a pair the Möbius invariants (σ1, σ2) of the fixed-point multipliers agree to …, and points of different pairs differ by at least …. Each tube joins the two points of a pair; the color marks the pair.

Why tubes. For small λ ≠ 0, Per5(λ) (a marked 5-cycle of multiplier λ) is a nearby curve on which each node is smoothed out into a thin neck. A neck joining two points of a connected surface adds a handle, so adds 1 to the genus. Heuristically genus Per5(λ) = genus Per5(0) + 10 = 1 + 10 = 11, and in general genus Pern(λ) = genus Pern(0) + NB(n)/2, where NB(n) is the number of bitransitive points. This assumes the nodes are the only change as λ → 0: no other singularities, and the punctures at infinity behave.

Status: CONJECTURE / heuristic. Not proved, not independently verified. Evidence so far: the 10 pairs (numerical); genus-11 slit-torus frames work for λ ∈ [−0.0625, 0], and at λ = −0.01 the torus part has τ = −0.000080 + 0.886957i, against 0.887394i for Per5(0). An independent test on Per3(λ) is planned. Open: the numbers of bitransitive points found for n = 3 … 9 are 2, 6, 20, 46, 128, 289, 690 (numerical); 289 is odd, so the pairing needs care at n = 8 (self-paired or degenerate points?).

The picture. The tubes are an immersed drawing of the necks and may cross each other. The genus is a property of the curve, not of this drawing.
