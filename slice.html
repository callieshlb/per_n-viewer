The real slice (a, b) ∈ ℝ²

f(z) = 1 + b/z + a/z², a, b real; marked critical point ω = 0 → ∞ → 1 → … (wj = fj(0)), free critical point ω′ = −2a/b. Every curve with a page is drawn where it meets this real plane: Pern(0) = {ω has exact period n} and PrePerk,n = {wk is the first periodic point, period n}. Per1 and Per2 do not meet this chart (w1 = ∞, w2 = 1 ≠ 0). Computed live in double precision; lower relations (k′ ≤ k, n′ | n, and ω periodic) are removed. Click near a curve: the point is snapped onto it, with a card and “Open in …”.
coordinates
megaPern (Caroline's script): f(z) = (z − x)(z − w)/z², w = (x + y − 1)/(x − 1), so ω = 0 → ∞ → 1 → y → …; Pern is drawn as an−1 = x (the orbit of y reaches the zero x of f). Same maps as the site chart: a = x w, b = −(x + w); the (x, y) plane double-covers the part of the (a, b) plane where z² + bz + a has real roots (x ↔ w).
background
Fate background: period of the attracting cycle that captures ω′ (light colors: 1 gray, 2 rose, 3 green, 4 blue, 5 sand, 6 violet …); dark: none found (300 iterates). Drag to pan; wheel, pinch, double-click (shift: out) or the buttons to zoom.
