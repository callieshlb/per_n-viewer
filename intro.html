The Pern(0) atlas

An interactive viewer for the curves Pern(0) of quadratic rational maps, n = 1, …, 8.
Open the atlas

What you are looking at

A quadratic rational map has two critical points. Pern(0) is the set of conjugacy classes of quadratic rational maps for which one critical point is periodic of period n. That periodic critical orbit is the marked cycle a0 → a1 → … → an−1 → a0 (a0 the critical point, a1 the critical value). The other critical point is the free critical point ω. Pern(0) is a punctured Riemann surface; each point of it is one map.

The atlas has two panes. The left pane is the parameter space Pern(0): each pixel is a map, colored by what ω does under that map. The right pane is the dynamical plane of the map you clicked: each pixel is a point z, colored by what its orbit does.

The genus, the number of punctures, of polynomial centers and of real circles of each curve are shown in the bar at the top of the atlas and in the status line.

Colors

  • Capture colors (blue, orange, green, …): ω eventually falls into the basin of the marked cycle. The color is the phase: it records which point of the marked cycle the orbit of ω is in step with, measured at the critical value a1 (phase 0 = the component of a1). The same colors are used in the dynamical plane and in the polynomial cards.
  • Dark gray: ω is attracted to a fixed point. These components contain the polynomial centers, where the map is conjugate to a polynomial z² + c.
  • Light gray: ω has its own attracting cycle of period ≥ 2. With “color components by period” these are tinted by that period.
  • Black: undecided within the iteration count (the bifurcation locus, or the Julia set in the right pane).

The pictures are computed on the graphics card in single precision at every redraw. Fine speckle where captures take very many iterates is numerical noise; click a point for the double-precision answer in the status line.

Markers

  • White dots: polynomial centers. A center is labeled by the even numerator k of its two characteristic angles k/(2ⁿ − 1). The violet dot is the center 2ⁿ⁻¹.
  • Red dots: punctures of the curve.
  • Yellow star / yellow dots: matings you computed (see below).
  • Real circles (off by default): the maps with real coefficients.
  • Gn edges and violet arrows (off by default): the graph Gn of tip capture edges between polynomial centers; a violet arrow points from a center to its neighbor(s) whose label has the most factors of 2.

Clicking a center opens its card: the Julia set of z² + c with its Hubbard tree and external rays. Clicking a puncture offers the Thurston obstruction diagram for it.

Tools

  • Mating (m): type f (either characteristic angle of a period-n center, or its label), Tab, type g (any angle), Enter. The mating f ⊔ g is computed by Thurston's pullback algorithm with the convention that the ray of angle θ of f is glued to the ray of angle −θ of g, and is placed in Pern as a yellow star. If the iteration degenerates the mating is reported as obstructed.
  • Mating vein: in the mating box, also mate f with the centers gi on the arc of the Mandelbrot set from z² to g (small yellow dots).
  • View in Perm (v): if ω has an attracting cycle of period m at the map you clicked, jump to the center of that component written as a point of Perm (the same rational map, with the other critical cycle marked). For m = n this is the involution exchanging the two n-cycles.
  • External rays (Per1): enter angles; the parameter and dynamical rays are drawn with their landing points.
  • Orbit mode (o), polynomial view (i: ω at ∞, like the Julia set of a polynomial), Hubbard tree (t) in the dynamical plane.
